Privacy Policy — Saleh

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Who uses this app

Saleh is a private personal assistant. It is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

When the owner grants access, Saleh can read and update the owner's Google Drive files, Google Calendar events and Gmail messages. It uses this access only to keep the owner's tasks, reminders and schedule up to date, and to prepare drafts the owner reviews.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to provide the assistant's features to the owner.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Access tokens are kept on the assistant's private server and are not published.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove Saleh's access at any time from Google Account → Third-party apps.

Contact

Questions about this policy: the support email shown on the app's Google consent screen.
