Saleh

A private personal assistant

Saleh is a personal assistant used by one person to organise his own tasks, reminders and calendar. It is not a public service and is not offered to other users.

With the owner's permission, Saleh reads and updates the owner's own Google Drive files, Google Calendar events and Gmail messages in order to keep his task list and reminders up to date.
